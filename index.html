Method, evidence requirements and test status

The review-only alternative is 4 × listing reviews, assuming 25% of purchases receive a review. That rate is an assumption, not a calibrated finding. Seven-/30-day figures use 4 × reviews published in that period.

The default lifetime formula is 4 × listing reviews + favorites ÷ 4. With 22 reviews and 349 favorites: the review-only baseline is 88, and your formula is 175.25 (displayed rounded). Favorites may come from non-buyers or overlap with reviewed purchases, so adding them can overestimate.

The previous shop-ratio calculation is now an inactive diagnostic in Evidence. A very low shop review rate could multiply each listing review into many assumed purchases. It no longer controls the default estimate.

The scenario range uses review multipliers 2× and 8×, with the selected favorite contribution held fixed. It is not a statistical confidence interval or a guaranteed lower/upper bound. Displayed values are capped by known shop sales when available.

Missing or zero listing reviews produce insufficient evidence, not fake zero sales. Review dates can lag purchases substantially. Twenty-two reviews support reviewed purchases, not exactly 22 total units sold. Actual seller sales are needed to judge accuracy.

Testing: calculations, evidence guards, API handling and simulated data are tested. Accuracy against actual seller orders has not been validated. No EHunt-level accuracy claim is made.

Install in desktop Chrome or Edge

↓ Download browser extension ZIP

  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder. The folder should contain manifest.json.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked, and choose the extracted folder.
  4. Keep a signed-in Niche Lab tab open. Use the account granted access to this website.
  5. Refresh both your Niche Lab and Etsy tabs. Metrics load automatically under visible product cards and near the product title. Use Details for the expanded panel.

This private extension is installed manually; it is not listed in the Chrome Web Store. Keep its extracted folder in place. It requires desktop Chrome or Edge.

Updating an installed extension

Extract this ZIP over the original extension folder, open your browser’s Extensions page, click Reload on Niche Lab, and refresh both your website and Etsy tabs. The new version is 1.3.2.

What appears automatically

  • Experimental lifetime, seven-day and 30-day review-based sales estimates, with insufficient-evidence labels where needed.
  • Public listing favorites, price, age, stock and lifetime favorites per day.
  • Shop total sales when Etsy returns that public field.
  • Seven-day favorite growth and net stock changes for tracked products with sufficient recorded history.
  • A Track daily activity button connected to this website’s cloud watchlist and daily collector.
Exact competitor product units sold and weekly sales are not disclosed by Etsy. The extension keeps exact sales unavailable and shows model estimates separately. Recent model periods use review dates, not purchase dates. Model accuracy against real orders is unvalidated.

No Etsy API key or password is packaged in the extension. Automatic display defaults to on and can be changed in the extension popup. Only visible products are loaded, up to 20 per page; additional products offer a Load button. Repeated lookups use a five-minute cache. Use Track daily to save a product for history. Interactive installation and Etsy-page layout still need checking in your browser; browser-extension controls were unavailable during development.

Etsy API connection

  1. Create or use your approved app at Etsy Developers.
  2. Configure ETSY_KEYSTRING and ETSY_SHARED_SECRET as this website’s server-side secrets. Credentials should never be put into browser code or pasted into chat.
  3. Redeploy after configuring secrets. Keyword searches will then use Etsy’s live API.

Your app allowance is 5 requests per second and 5,000 per day. Searches are paced, repeated data may be cached, and Etsy enforces the account-wide quota. This private website is protected by your ChatGPT account. API access still depends on Etsy app approval and permissions.

What the numbers mean

  • Trend velocity = favorites ÷ max(listing age in days, 1). It measures lifetime favorite pace.
  • Shop sales are shop-wide totals. They do not establish sales of one product.
  • Inventory changes can indicate sales, edits, or restocking. They are uncertain proxies.
  • There is no universal 999-unit digital starting inventory.
  • Seven-day deltas need an observation from seven days earlier. More history improves context, not certainty.
  • Cloud watchlists support up to 50 keywords and 1,000 stored products. Daily keyword discovery adds up to 20 relevant products per keyword and continues following them.
  • Snapshots are unique per product per UTC date. Missing days stay missing; errors and observation age are shown in the Watchlist tab.
